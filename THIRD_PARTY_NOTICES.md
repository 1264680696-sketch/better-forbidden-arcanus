## Forbidden and Arcanus

Original project: https://github.com/stal111/Forbidden-Arcanus

Author: stal111 and the Forbidden and Arcanus contributors.

This addon depends on Forbidden and Arcanus 2.6.1 and adapts selected historical implementations, textures, and models. Source revisions include:

- `189c3ca451af186415e3e2f3f0fbb4d8226819b6` (1.20.x): zombie arms and Draco Arcanus recipes.
- `a915c22ab3bed69f3c27ae0d2f278158d102a6c5` (1.16): Runic Tenebris devices, runic darkstone, and legacy book artwork.
- `96ecfcacabd7c7c1d97706c962e8c836e70766d1` (1.14): Runic Battleaxe, skull, and associated resources.

The 1.16 and 1.20 source revisions declare `All Rights Reserved` in their mod metadata. Attribution alone does not grant redistribution rights.
